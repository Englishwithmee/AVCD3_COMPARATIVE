A · Comparative

Comparatives

Compare two people, places or things. So sánh hơn giữa hai đối tượng.

Short: adjective + -er + than
My room is smaller than yours.
Today is hotter than yesterday.
Long: more + adjective + than
This hotel is more expensive than that one.

Irregular: good → better; bad → worse; far → farther/further.

B · Equal / Not equal

(not) as ... as

Use as + adjective + as when two things are equal; use not as + adjective + as when they are different.

as + adjective + as
The subway is as crowded as the bus.
not as + adjective + as
My room is not as big as yours.
São Paulo is not as famous as Moscow.

Do not use an -er ending or more inside the as ... as structure.

C · Superlative

Superlatives

Compare one person or thing with a group of three or more. So sánh nhất.

Short: the + adjective + -est
It is the tallest building in town.
Long: the most + adjective
It is the most interesting museum in the city.

Irregular: good → the best; bad → the worst; far → the farthest/furthest.

D · Spelling & patterns

Quick spelling rules

+ er / + est: old → older → oldest
Final -e: nice → nicer → nicest
Consonant-vowel-consonant: big → bigger → biggest
Consonant + y: busy → busier → busiest
Long adjectives: beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful

Use than in a comparative; usually use the before a superlative. Some two-syllable adjectives allow more than one pattern, so check standard usage.
